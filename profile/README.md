# Kopf Studio

**Reliable integrations, internal tools, and operational automation.**

Kopf Studio builds software for businesses whose teams spend too much time
moving data between disconnected systems or managing repetitive work manually.
We focus on clear API contracts, safe retries, operational visibility, and
software that remains maintainable as the business grows.

## Who We Help

Retailers, small e-commerce operations, and teams that need their sales,
back-office, and operational systems to work together.

Examples of problems we can help address:

- **A fashion or cosmetics store** needs to bring marketplace and web-store
  orders into one operational view, reducing manual copying between systems.
- **A specialty retailer** needs orders synchronized with an ERP so staff can
  track what was accepted, what failed, and what needs attention.
- **A small e-commerce operation** needs a way to recover failed order syncs
  without creating duplicate orders when a request is retried.

These are illustrative scenarios, not client case studies or claims of existing
platform integrations. Each project starts by checking the systems' APIs,
constraints, and operational requirements.

## What We Build

- Integrations between APIs, sales channels, and back-office systems
- Internal tools for order handling, exceptions, and operational visibility
- Workflow automation with explicit failure handling and safe recovery
- Backend services and maintainable foundations for business applications

## Engineering Work

### [GrantLedger](https://github.com/kopfstudio/grantledger)

Multi-tenant SaaS billing infrastructure with a completed platform baseline,
idempotent operations, replay-safe webhooks, asynchronous invoice processing,
and observability. Includes a guided self-hosted demo and deployment validation tooling.

### [Authentication API](https://github.com/gabedalmolin/auth-api-node)

A production-oriented service by
[Gabriel Dalmolin](https://github.com/gabedalmolin), demonstrating session lifecycle
management, refresh-token replay detection, rate limiting, and integration testing.
Local setup and API documentation are available in the repository.

### [Marketplace Sync API](https://github.com/gabedalmolin/marketplace-sync-api) — In Development

Being developed toward a commercial demo of reliable order ingestion and external
synchronization. Application and database foundations are implemented; webhook
workflows, order endpoints, and external synchronization are still planned.

### [Abaris](https://github.com/kopfstudio/abaris) — Research

Architectural work on reproducible vulnerability experiments and patch-behavior
validation. Currently in the documentation and foundation phase, with no working
CLI or supported execution backend.

These repositories show engineering work and project status; they are not claims
of customer deployments or hosted service availability.
